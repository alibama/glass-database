Glass ID

A one-of-a-kind ID for your piece, like a serial number for handmade glass. Walk around your piece with your phone and it gets its Glass ID. A live rating climbs as you go; when it reads Strong, give the ID to the piece. This is an alpha: thresholds are still being calibrated on real pieces, so treat a verdict as evidence and tell us on the Discord what you saw.

Your Glass ID is a fingerprint of the glass itself, stored as numbers, not photos. New here? Watch the walkthrough (a short on YouTube).
