    № 01 Usage

    Who used what.

    Self-hosted and cookieless: no third party, no raw IP stored. A visitor is a per-day rotating hash, so the visitor counts are approximate and nobody is tracked across days. Do-Not-Track is honoured. Country appears only when a GeoLite2 database is configured.
