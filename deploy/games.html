№ 02 How it works

Make it. Buy it. Vote.

A time-boxed call for pieces. Makers enter fingerprinted work, collectors scan what they bought to claim it, and everyone spends a few votes.

Getting in takes a Google sign-in and nothing to install. Glowtbook (your private registry) and Verify (the scanner) are web pages that work on a phone. Voting needs the same sign-in and nothing else.

For makers

  • Fingerprint

    Log the piece in Glowtbook and capture its fingerprint — a few photos off the pipe that let a phone recognise this pumpkin later.

  • Publish

    Push it to the public registry. Once it's approved it has a page, a manifest, a Content Credential and a scan-to-verify link.

  • Enter

    Enter it in the season with your studio, the edition size and where to buy. Then tell your people.

For collectors

  • Buy

    Get a piece from a shop in the season — at the studio, the market, the website. The maker gets paid; that's the point.

  • Scan

    Open Verify, point your phone at the piece, and let the confidence climb. 70+ across a few angles is a match.

  • Claim

    Claim it to your collection. It's now an authenticated piece with a real provenance trail — yours.

  • Vote

    Spend your votes on the entries you'd take home. Swap them as often as you like until voting closes.

For shops & hosts

  • List

    Get your studio on the record: submit it in Explore, or claim the listing if it's already there.

  • Host

    Running an ornament day or a pop-up? Open Glowtbook → Events, make the event, print the QR for the table. Buyers register their piece on the spot.

  • Enter

    Every maker at your table enters their own pieces from their own Glowtbook — up to five each, one legendary. The studio board adds them up.

  • Ask

    Want a season themed around your shop or show? Propose it on the Discord.

Rules of play

Makers

  1. Enter your own work, made for this season.
  2. Up to five entries per season.
  3. Fingerprint a piece before you enter it. That is what makes it Uncommon or better, and what the measured awards use.
  4. One legendary per maker per season: an edition of one, fingerprinted, with its legend written. Don't say which piece it is — the collector finds out when they claim it.
  5. You can't vote for your own pieces.
  6. "Solo" means made without an assistant on the pipe. On your honour.

Collectors

  1. One pick per award, plus your People's choice votes.
  2. A claim needs the piece in your hands (a fingerprint match) or the maker's confirmation.
  3. One claim per piece per person.
  4. Points: common 1 · uncommon 2 · rare 5 · legendary 20.
  5. Complete a set — one piece from every studio in the season — for the badge.

Rarity

  1. Common any approved entry.
  2. Uncommon fingerprinted.
  3. Rare fingerprinted, edition of three or fewer.
  4. Legendary the maker's one-of-one, revealed when it's claimed.

Everyone: the game never handles money — buy from the maker. Admins can remove entries or claims that break the rules; disputes go to the Discord.

    № 08 Why this exists

    A reason to buy from a working shop.

    Independent glass artists need collectors who come back, and collectors want to know the piece on the shelf is the piece the maker made. Seasons are a reason to buy from a working shop this month, a way to prove what you own without a certificate in a drawer, and — quietly — a census of who is making what, where. No cash prizes, no entry fees: recognition, badges, a homepage feature, and a growing record of real glass in real collections. Data stays open under CC BY 4.0.
