    № 06 Glass ID · Alpha — for testers

    A one-of-a-kind ID for handmade glass.

    Like a serial number, except nothing is stamped on the piece. Set it on the Photo Mat, walk around it with your phone until the bar fills up, and it has a Glass ID. Later, anyone can point a phone at the piece and see who made it.

    Sign in once, or pair your phone from Glowtbook with a QR, pick the piece, and walk around it until the bar fills up. The printable Photo Mat gives true size and colour.

    The Glass ID is saved straight onto the piece in your Glowtbook, not lost in an export. An unsaved session is kept on your phone until you save it.

    Point a phone at the piece and it is recognised. A likely match is strong evidence, not proof, and the check travels with the piece's story.

    Alpha, open to testers. Registering and checking work today and are strongest on pieces with distinctive colour and detail; the thresholds are still being calibrated on real pieces. If you have a shelf of your own work and a phone, you're exactly who we need.

    № 07 Glowtbook · your studio notebook

    From the hot shop to the archive.

    The making is fleeting; the record shouldn't be. Glowtbook is your studio notebook: log a piece, photograph it off the pipe, record where it has been. Private until you publish. Works on your phone.

    Open GlowtbookYour private notebook and the list of your pieces.
    Enter a collecting eventPut one of your registered pieces into this season's event.

    What travels with a piece

    • i.Its story: made, sold, found, checked, with dates and names
    • ii.Its Glass ID, so any phone can recognise it later
    • iii.Its pictures, with the maker's name sealed in
    • iv.Its style, in words that museums and Wikipedia also use
    • v.A listing that works with a screen reader from the start

    The fine print: originals are archived as BagIt bags, pictures carry Content Credentials (C2PA), and style terms are aligned to Getty AAT and Wikidata.

    Blowing Glass in Lima

    Lima is an easy place to melt glass and a hard place to sell it — cheap piped gas, a mining economy that digs up every oxide in a glass batch, and a city of eleven million buying its glass from somewhere else. A hot shop here costs about $380,000 to build and $350,000 a year to run, and the constraint is not energy but demand.

    № 10 About

    Craft and capital, in one open record.

    The Glass Database was started by Jason Bauer to look at the glass world from both sides at once: the craft and the capital. What a studio costs to build and run, who taught whom, where the work goes and what it sells for. It picked up attention, and it is now an open-source project open to glassblowers, collectors and researchers interested in the emerging, evolving space of studio art glass.

    Founder

    Jason Robert Bauer

    Artist and founder of the Glass Database (b. 1986). Trained as a furnace glassmaker and a lifelong craft enthusiast, he works mainly with light, and also with sound and time, through a futurist aesthetic designed to shift the viewer's perception. He works intuitively, often building from parts made on separate occasions. With his wife and partner Romina Gonzales he co-founded J & R Bauer Inc. and The Third Room. Based between north-western New York and western Peru.

    Maintainer

    Anson Parker

    Keeps the furnace running at Raging Buffalo Glass and has spent almost thirty years building open-source software for libraries.

    How it's run

    • i.Data under CC BY 4.0, with attribution kept and removal on request; code under Apache 2.0 on GitHub.
    • ii.Nothing is public until it's reviewed: every row passes an approval gate, and names and birth dates stay out of the per-row views.
    • iii.Standards where they exist: C2PA for credentials, BagIt and OAIS for the archive, SKOS aligned to Getty AAT and Wikidata for style.
    • iv.Conversation happens on the Discord; the roadmap and changelog live in the repository.
    • v.We are building our own AI rather than renting one: open models on machines members lend, on a private network behind the site, so the reading of your photos and video stays privileged to community members and nothing you upload goes to a commercial AI service. How it runs shows what is live and what is next.
