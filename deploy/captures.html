    № 01 Captures

    Every capture session, kept.

    When someone taps Send for analysis in Register Your Piece or Check a Piece, the session lands here: the photos the app kept, the numbers behind them, the registered pieces it was compared with and their scores, the decision the person took, what the Photo Mat gave, and the phone and app version. This is the calibration set for the matching: open a session, download it, or pull it down with the command below.
