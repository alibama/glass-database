№ 05 · 3D view · prototype, for testers

Turn it in your hands.

A Gaussian splat is a 3D photograph: it keeps the shine and the colour shifts that make glass hard to photograph, and you can rotate it. Your phone does the work; this page just shows it and gives you a link to share.

Loading Drag to turn · pinch or wheel to zoom

What this is not. A splat is a picture. It is never signed into a piece's Content Credentials and never used as a fingerprint. Anyone can upload a splat of anything, so treat it as a maker's own rendering of their work, nothing more.

How to make one

  1. Scan it on your phone

    Scaniverse (free, iOS and Android) trains the splat on the phone itself: pick Splat, orbit the piece slowly at two or three heights with the light behind you, then export Gaussian Splat → PLY. Polycam works too. On a laptop, Brush or OpenSplat turn a folder of photos into one.

  2. Crop it

    Open the file in SuperSplat in your browser, delete the table and the room, keep the piece, and export as PLY or SPLAT. Under 150 MB uploads here; under 30 MB loads fast on a phone.

  3. Upload and share

    Sign in, pick the piece it belongs to, upload. You get a link that opens this page with your piece turning. One splat per piece; uploading again replaces it.
