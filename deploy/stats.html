    № 01 The numbers

    What the record holds.

    A voluntary census of who makes glass, where, and what they have made, counted live: the pieces and their Glass IDs, the people and studios, the collecting events, what has gone to the wiki and been matched on Wikidata, and the makers the record knows best. Open data, CC BY 4.0.

    № 02 The record

    Pieces, people, places.

    Only approved, public rows count. A registered piece carries a Glass ID any phone can check; a credentialed one carries Content Credentials on its photographs.

    Across every collecting event the site has run.

    № 04 The wiki and Wikidata

    On the wider record.

    The public, durable half of the data lives on the site's own Wikibase, with every item keyed by its Glass Database ID and cross-referenced to Wikidata where the same thing exists there.

    Items by kind

    The Wikidata lookup

    Known: an id that was already on the row. Matched: the lookup found exactly one. Unsure: namesakes waiting for a reviewer, or for the Resolve tool's suggestion. Confirmed and rejected: a person decided.

    № 05 Makers

    Who the record knows best.

    Every named maker scored the same way: forty points for the bio, twenty for pieces, ten for the share with a Glass ID, ten for collecting events, ten for votes and honours, ten for links out. Click a name for the card.
