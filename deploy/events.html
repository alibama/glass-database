Events

Found a treasure at the table? Start a collection you can prove.

Makers host events — ornament days, pop-ups, demos. The piece you take home joins your collection the same afternoon, with its story attached: made by the maker, found by you, ready to fingerprint and prove.

How it works

For collectors

Find

Find a piece at the event — the table, the gallery, the demo. The maker gets paid; that's the point.

Claim it

Scan the QR on the table or open Add to your collection. Sign in, say what you found, snap a photo. A minute, standing up.

Maker confirms

The maker sees it in Glowtbook and confirms it came from their table — usually the same day.

In your collection

The registry publishes it with provenance: made by the maker at the event, collected by you. Your name stays private unless you choose otherwise.

Prove it

It's already in your Glowtbook. Capture its fingerprint and any phone can prove this piece is the one in your collection — not one like it.

For makers

Host

Open Glowtbook → Events and add the sale: venue, date, what you're making. It works the moment you save it.

QR on the table

Print the registration link and its QR. Buyers scan it with the piece in their hand.

Approve

After the event, approve the registrations in bulk — or reject the one that wasn't yours.

Published

The site reviews and publishes them. Each piece becomes part of someone's collection with your name on it — and a reason to come back for the next one.

Provenance starts at the till.

A certificate in a drawer gets lost; a record made at the moment of sale doesn't. Hosting an event costs nothing: add it in Glowtbook, print the QR, and every piece that leaves the table can carry your name, the date and the place for as long as it exists. The collectors come back, and — quietly — it's a census of who is making what, where, and what it sells for. Data stays open under CC BY 4.0.
