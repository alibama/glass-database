Glass Database

    № 00 — The mission

    The mission of Glass Database is to bring about the awareness and ability for independent artists to build sustainable careers using glass as a material.

    We are in the business of growing the craft despite the dreaded high cost of the material… we are inclusive and want to speak to all specialities in glass, from stained glass to torch, cast, slump, furnace, etc.

    № 02 About

    Craft and capital, in one open record.

    The Glass Database was started by Jason Bauer to look at the glass world from both sides at once: the craft and the capital. It is now an open-source project, maintained by Anson Parker, open to glassblowers, collectors and researchers in the emerging, evolving space of studio art glass.

    How it's run

    • i.Data under CC BY 4.0, with attribution kept and removal on request; code under Apache 2.0 on GitHub.
    • ii.Nothing is public until it's reviewed: every row passes an approval gate, and names and birth dates stay out of the per-row views.
    • iii.We are building our own AI rather than renting one: open models on machines members lend, on a private network behind the site, so the reading of your photos and video stays privileged to community members. How it runs shows what is live and what is next.

    Blowing Glass in Lima

    Lima is an easy place to melt glass and a hard place to sell it — cheap piped gas, a mining economy that digs up every oxide in a glass batch, and a city of eleven million buying its glass from somewhere else. A hot shop here costs about $380,000 to build and $350,000 a year to run, and the constraint is not energy but demand.
