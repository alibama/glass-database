The master stays yours

When you contribute, the full-quality original and any private values stay with you. A condensed, public copy goes to the registry, carrying:

  • a preservation-grade archive of the originals (BagIt)
  • a downscaled public rendition — images and video
  • Content Credentials (C2PA) signed into the image
  • a review step before anything appears in public

Built by the community, corrected by it

Listings come from makers and organizers. Nothing goes public until it's been reviewed, and anything can be corrected or removed on request.
