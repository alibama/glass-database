    № 07 Studio docs · Build the equipment

    A furnace, an annealer and a glory hole, part by part.

    Loading the parts lists…

    Safety and code. Nothing on this page is an engineering drawing or a permit set. Gas trains follow the National Fuel Gas Code (NFPA 54) and the furnace standard (NFPA 86); feeders and controls follow the National Electrical Code; both are installed and inspected by licensed people to the edition in force where you build. The amperages, orifice drills and pipe sizes here are the design figures for those people to check, with the arithmetic shown beside them.

    01 Your options

    Pick the service and the fuel.

    Every list below filters to your choices; the sizing tables recompute. The parts that do not depend on a choice stay put.

    A 208 V single-phase building (two legs of a 208 V wye) uses the single-phase parts with the 208 V arithmetic: about 13% more current for the same power.

    02 Sizing

    The arithmetic the parts lists rest on.

    Branch circuits at 125% of a continuous load; furnace element strings chosen so new elements run below line voltage and old ones still reach full power; annealer coils kept under 22 W/in²; burner orifices from Q = 1658.5 · A · 0.9 · √(h ÷ SG); gas pipe from the code's low-pressure table.
