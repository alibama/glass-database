    № 01 How it runs

    Small machines, kept by the field.

    The Glass Database runs on one small rented server today. We are building the next layer ourselves, out of machines the community lends, so that the AI that reads your photos and video belongs to the people who make and collect the glass, and stays available to members rather than rented from a vendor. This page says what is running now, what is next, and how to help.

    Join the DiscordWhere the infrastructure gets talked through and machines get added.
    Read the runbookThe operator's version: services, ports, the overlay, backups.

    № 02 The shape of it

    One public door, a private workshop behind it.

    Everything you touch goes through the public server. The machines that do the heavy reading sit on a private network behind it, reachable only by that server, never by the open internet. Solid lines run today; red dashed lines are what we are building.

    № 04 Our own AI

    Why build it instead of renting it.

    Reading a photo of a pumpkin is cheap; reading every photo and video that arrives, forever, is not, and sending them all to a vendor means the vendor holds the field's record of itself. We would rather run open models on machines members lend, and keep what they produce privileged to members.

    Your photos stay in the house

    Nothing you upload is sent to a commercial AI service. Models run on machines we or members control, on a private network, and the record says which model and which machine read each piece.

    Members first

    Captions, checks, searches and 3D Photos made this way are for signed-in members and the makers whose work it is. The public record stays public and CC BY; the extra reading is ours.

    Lend, don't donate

    A lent machine stays yours. It receives work only from the server, you can see what it did, and you can pull it out of the network whenever you like.

    № 05 Get involved

    Four ways to help.

    Everything starts on the Discord; that is where machines are added and where the page you are reading gets corrected.

    Talk

    Join the Discord

    Say what you make, what you have, what you want the database to do. Infrastructure has its own channel.

    Open the Discord     Lend

    Lend a machine

    A Linux or Mac box with 16 GB or more that is idle most of the day. You install two things; we hand you a one-time join key on the Discord; you can watch it work and leave any time.

    Ask for a join key     Test

    Register pieces

    Every piece registered and published is something the first worker will read. Testers steer the thresholds and the words it uses.

    Register a piece     Build

    Contribute code

    Python, FastAPI, Streamlit, plain HTML. The runbook in the repository says how the network is laid out and what is next.

    GitHub

    Lending a machine, in a sentence: you install the open-source network client and a model server, join with a key we give you, and the server sends your machine work when it is on. It never serves the public, never sees anyone else's private notebook, and holds nothing you cannot delete. Details, including what we log, are in the runbook.
